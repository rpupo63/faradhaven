# DM Guide: Otto — Stoner Elixirist of the Copper Veins

**Use:** Drop-in NPC for the **Copper Veins** (the Sub-Station). Otto is a local **Elixirist**—a brewer who works in **Emax** (liquid **Vital Spark**) and never serves a gift without a price: every bottle has an **Elixir** and a **Flux**. He is not a cartoon; he is **slow**, **generous**, and **dangerous to underestimate** because the city still treats **Electro-Vitalic** craft as **anomalous resonance** bait.

**Lore anchors:** [`lore-notes/copper-veins.md`](../lore-notes/copper-veins.md), [`lore-notes/world-overview.md`](../lore-notes/world-overview.md). **Class reference:** `backend/seed/faradhaven_classes/elixirist.go` (Faradhaven Elixirist).

---

## I. Who He Is

**Otto** (he/him; pick a surname if you need paperwork—*Copper*, *Vein*, *Slag* all work) is a **Galvanized** or **Homan-adjacent** alchemist who settled into the Veins because the **Static Haze** and the **Copper Dirge** feel like home. He talks like the fog thinks: long pauses, circular stories, and sudden precision when someone is bleeding or burning.

He presents as a **“stoner”** in the table sense: perpetually **mellow**, **unhurried**, often nursing a **vapor** or a **smoke-tonic** cloud (see **Signature brews**). In-world, frame this as **Odor-forward** formulas and **Homan-style** “Party” chemistry—**not** as a joke about real-world substance use at the table unless your group explicitly wants that tone. The Veins already describe **Mixologists** spiking **Vitalic-Amrita** with **runoff**; Otto is the guy who **knows the recipe** and **names the hangover**.

---

## II. Where He Lives (Copper Veins)

Otto’s **spot** is almost never a proper shop:

- **Likely:** A **Faraday-mesh** basement off the **Slag-Pits**, a **Retro-Mojo** side room that smells like old diner fryers and copper, or a **Homan circuit** “potion bar” **after-hours** kitchen where the real kettle lives behind a false wall.
- **Politics:** He is **small potatoes** for the **Mayor’s office** (or whatever replaced it), but **large signal** for **ACC** if he brews **on camera**. The Veins still run **Protective Custody** → **Union** labs and **anomalous resonance** patrols; Otto’s whole life is **noise discipline**—when to vent smoke, when to dampen **Flux**, when to eat the cost and use **Flux Dampener** so a patron does not scream and draw **evaluation**.

Tie him to existing infrastructure from the Veins note:

| Thread | How Otto lives it |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Homans & Mixology** | He is either **respected** as a flavor savant or **resented** as a **purist** who will not spike amrita “hard enough” for the Party crowd. |
| **Temporarily Embarrassed Humans** | Retro clubs hire him for **“authentic”** tonics; he finds them exhausting but pays rent. |
| **Surface-Outcasts** | He needs **clean adjuncts** sometimes; smugglers **tax** him in **Shunts**, favors, or **humiliation**—play that friction. |
| **Underground sanctuary** | If you use **Healing Centers** / **Kinetic Front** safe houses in your campaign, Otto is a **natural fence** for **non-tracked** stabilization—at the cost of **Flux** on someone’s nerves. |

## III. Personality & Table Voice

- **Pace:** Speaks **slow**; interrupt him and he **does not speed up**—he **finishes the thought** anyway. Good for buying time in scenes or annoying impatient NPCs.
- **Morality:** **Soft** until you threaten **his people** or **his kettle**. Then the **Flux** side of his art stops being theoretical.
- **Humor:** Dry, **accidental poetry**; he names bottles like bands. He **does not** explain the dual brew unless someone is **about to drink**.
- **Secret depth:** He remembers **who refused Flux** and who **took both**—that is the Elixirist’s worldview in miniature.

---

## IV. Elixirist Mechanics (What the DM Needs)

Otto uses the **Elixirist** chassis: **Intelligence**-primary, **Emax Reserves**, **Prepared Formulas**, **Dual Brew** (Elixir then Flux; willing targets may **refuse Flux** and **halve Elixir**), **Flux Siphon** (when his **Flux** deals damage, he **recovers Emax**—unless Flux was **fully suppressed**), **Flux Dampener** (short-rest pool; suppress Flux, **no Siphon**), **Overdrive** (long-rest pool; doubles formula dice on **both** tracks).

**Suggested default build for Otto as a **support contact** (adjust level to taste):**

| Knob | Suggestion |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Level** | **5–7** (Tier II online; established but not apex). |
| **Archetype** | **The Equilibrist** — “balance” matches his **stoner philosopher** vibe, and **Perfect Equilibrium** rewards allies who **accept both** Elixir and Flux (narratively: **trust the process**). **The Purist** works if you want him gentler and more **medic**; **The Flux Merchant** if he is **shadier**. |
| **Prepared formulas (flavor)** | **Smoke Tonic** (obscurement + blind risk), **Numbing Agent** (calm the mind / sting the nerves), **Vital Tonic** (healing with teeth), **Shock Vial** when cornered. |
| **Risk at the table** | If Otto **throws** at an enemy, **only Flux** applies on a hit—classic bar fight tool. If the **Mayor’s** people measure **resonance**, **Overdrive** or a bad night of **uncounted Flux** is your **raid hook**. |

**Copper Veins reminder:** Magic-as-research means **any** public display of **Electro-Vitalic** weirdness can become **detention**—Otto’s **smoke** might be **cover** or **evidence**, depending on who watches.

## V. Signature Brews & Props

Use these as **flavor objects**, not full stat blocks:

- **“Copper Nap”** — A **Smoke Tonic** variant: thick **pea-soup** cloud **heavily obscured** in a small radius; **Flux** might **blind** the careless. Fits the **Industrial Gothic** fog language from the world overview.
- **“Veins Honesty”** — A **Vitalic Surge** or **Vital Tonic** served with **no Dampener**: heal **and** hurt—**Equilibrist** patrons **love** the resonance; tourists **panic**.
- **“Grounding Tea”** — **Flux Dampener** in fiction: tastes **flat**, costs Otto **resources**, saves someone’s **liver** or **reputation**.

---

## VI. Hooks (Sessions)

1. **The Union wants a sample** — **Dr. Mire Cault**-style observer offers **amrita waste** for **ledger access**; Otto asks the PCs to **run interference** or **forge a batch** that reads **harmless** on scanners.
2. **A Homan star mixologist** (cf. *Nine-Oh* types in other guides) **blacklists** Otto’s profile unless he **spikes harder**—**Flux** vs **conscience**.
3. **Someone OD’d on the Flux** — not “real-world drug” mandatory; in-setting, **necrotic** **Flux** and **industrial adjunct** **Poisoned** states are enough. Otto needs **Healing Center** access or **Volt-Stalker** silence.
4. **The kettle is hot** — **Protective Custody** knocks; PCs must **hide the brew**, **hide Otto**, or **bribe the right** copper.

## VII. Character Sheet Stats (Fixed Build)

Ability scores assume **standard array** + **+2 Intelligence**, **+1 Constitution** (Galvanized / Homan-adjacent lineage—swap bonuses if your table uses a different race).

### Saves & Skills

**Saving throws (class):** Constitution **+5**, Intelligence **+7**

**Skill proficiencies (class, choose two—Otto’s picks):** **Arcana** +7, **Investigation** +7

If you want more “street chemist” flavor, swap **Investigation** for **Insight** +4 (**WIS**) or **Medicine** +4 (**WIS** raw 5e) / **Medicine** +7 if your table keys Medicine off **INT** for Elixirists.

**Tool proficiencies:** Alchemist’s supplies, poisoner’s kit

**Passive Investigation** 17; **Passive Perception** 11 (or 14 if you make **Perception** a proficiency instead of **Investigation**).

### Elixirist Resources (Level 6)

From class progression: **Emax Reserves** **6** / 6, **Prepared Formulas** **3** (pick any three known formulas), **Flux Dampener** **1** / Short Rest, **Overdrive** **1** / Long Rest.

**Formula save DC** (when a formula calls for **8 + PB + INT**): **15**

**Spell / formula attack** (if ever needed): **+7** (**INT** + PB)

### Equipment (Typical)

Leather armor (or studded), **alchemist’s supplies**, **poisoner’s kit**, **dagger**, **light crossbow** and 20 bolts *or* second dagger, **healer’s kit**, empty vials, explorer’s or street pack as fits your campaign.

### Equilibrist Features in Play (Reminder)

**Perfect Equilibrium (3rd):** When a creature **willingly** takes **both** Elixir and Flux (no **Flux Dampener**, no **halved** Elixir from refusing Flux), they get **+4** (**INT** mod) on the **next** attack, check, or save before end of **next** turn.
