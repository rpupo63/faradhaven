# Episode 5 Guide: Brew & Brass (Copper Veins)

**Neighborhood:** The Copper Veins (the Sub-Station) — same streets, same strata: nostalgic **Galvanized**, **Homan** mixologists, **Surface-Outcast** smugglers, **Volt-Stalkers**, and **Kinetic Front** safe-house culture. **Canon for this table:** the **Mayor is dead — killed by the PCs** — so the old corporate-revolution **facade** is cracked; **ACC** and **Current Cartels** still want order, samples, and revenue, but nobody credible has replugged the myth yet.

**Premise:** The PCs are the **makers** of an illegal brew operation: they **source ingredients**, **run the kettle**, and **move product** through a cramped front (kitchen, back room, or speakeasy counter) like a restaurant during a rush — while **Protective Custody**, **anomalous-resonance** patrols, and **Volt-Stalkers** (some sympathetic, some informants) try to close the loop. **Heat** is worse when your name is already tied to the assassination rumor-mill.

**Front (suggested):** *The Grounding Point* — a Faraday-shielded basement in the Slag-Pits, or any “retro” Galvanized haunt. Without a Mayor to stage-manage blame, fronts survive on **bribes**, **shields**, and **which faction** currently finds you useful.

**Product (street name):** **Veins Cut** — the umbrella brand for whatever’s in the bottles tonight. Regulars order “a Cut”; dealers and ledgers use the same name whether the batch is **Homan High**–style or **Retro-Mojo**–style.

## I. Why the Veins Matter (Lore Anchors)

Use these when you need a quick read on a patron, a bribe, or a raid justification:

| Thread | Play at the table |
| :--- | :--- |
| **After the Mayor** | No single throat to choke — **ACC**, cartels, and street bosses each imply a different “law.” Raids may be **political** (silence witnesses) not just routine shakedowns. |
| **Temporarily Embarrassed Humans** | Nostalgia money: Old World flavors, “Retro-Mojo” aesthetics, stories of the Surface — **low Heat** if you keep it deniable. |
| **Homans** | **Mixology culture** — they *expect* spiked, industrial-adjacent brews; big spenders, **high Heat** if the party gets messy or magic leaks. |
| **Surface-Outcasts** | Smugglers who can **pass** topside; best pipeline for **clean** grain, medicine-adjacent inputs, and off-books ledgers — often arrogant, always negotiating. |
| **Judicial reality** | “Protective Custody” still funnels bodies to **Union** labs; **Electro-Vitalic magic** still draws “anomalous resonance” attention → **evaluation** detention — only the **excuse** on the warrant changes. |

**Objective (flexible):** Stay solvent, keep the batch legal enough for the street and illegal enough for the books — and decide whether you’re feeding Julian Vane’s agenda, the **Kinetic Front**, or only yourselves.

## II. Setting: The Sub-Station in Play

Ground scenes in **brass, recycled neon, Faraday mesh**, and the smell of **slag-adjunct** cutting through fermentation. These beats come straight from how the Veins are built to run:

| Layer | What it is | After the Mayor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mayor’s office (vacuum)** | Was a **figurehead “populist”** regime: **controlled opposition** let people blow off steam with harmless topside sabotage so **Current Cartels** stayed safe. | No one credible replugs the myth yet — **street bosses**, **ACC**, and **cartel** cutouts each offer a different story of who “authorizes” what. |
| **Information blackout** | State doctrine: the **Overworld** is pure hatred and doom so **Galvanized** never integrate and no one researches Underground ethics above-board. | The blackout is **fraying**; rumors about the Surface, the killing, and **ACC** leak through smugglers and retro clubs. |
| **ACC conduit** | The old administration traded **live Galvanized** to **Oscillating Union** science for backing and supplies. | Whoever inherits the **conduit** still wants **samples** and **data** — “Protective Custody” and clinic referrals didn’t vanish with the Mayor. |
| **Retro-Mojo districts** | **Temporarily Embarrassed Humans** cling to **Old World** habits from just before the **Vitalic Revolution** — dated fashion, recreated **90s-norm** social rituals. | Good cover for a front: “just another nostalgia joint” — until someone wants **Intel** or a **story** that pins the PCs. |
| **Homan nightlife** | **Abstinent hedonists**: no reproduction, strict **anti-intimacy** norms; **the Party** is chemical. **Mixologists** spike **Vitalic-Amrita** with **runoff / industrial byproduct** — toxic to humans, **Vital Spark** hits for Homans. | Your brew line is either **competition** or **collaboration**; a bad batch or a moral line crossed can turn **celebrity mixologists** against you. |
| **Surface-Outcast layer** | **Pure humans** here from debt, crime, or exile; **smugglers** **pass** topside and move **clean food** and **high-grade medical** goods. | They often **merchant-prince** the Underground while quietly despising **Synthetics** — negotiate hard, insult carefully. |
| **Judicial funnel** | **Protective Custody** = **Union** intake; **Electro-Vitalic** display triggers **anomalous resonance** → **evaluation** → **permanent detention** for study. | Warrants still say “care” and “stability”; only the **signature** changes. |
| **Underground sanctuary** | **Volt-Stalkers** (sympathetic guards) + **Kinetic Front** keep **safe spaces**; **Healing Centers** are rare places without **limiter-inhibitors** and **trackers**. | The Front may want your basement as a **meet**; a **Healing Center** contact is worth more than gold when someone **ODs** or **resonates**. |

**Quick location tags:** *Slag-Pits* (shielded basements, waste-adjacent), *Retro strip* (Galvanized clubs, faux-Surface diners), *Homan circuit* (mixology venues, “potion” bars), *smuggler docks* (ledger houses, clean-grain silos), *clinic façades* (Union-tied evaluation), *unmarked wards* (true Healing Centers).

## IV. The Core Loop: Brew Logistics (Restaurant Night)

Treat each session (or each “service”) as a **service window**: prep → line → floor → closeout, with **inventory** and **Heat** as your twin budgets.

### A. Procurement (Back of house, before doors open)

*Skills: Streetwise (Veins), Survival (Dead-Circuit / waste zones), or a smuggler’s contact*

| Ingredient | Quality | Copper Veins angle | Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vitalic-Amrita** (spike base) | High | ACC lab leaks, Homan dealers, “waste” from Union-tied clinics | Traces point at **ACC** / whoever is **claiming** the old Mayor’s conduit this week |
| **Old World grain / malt** | High | Surface-Outcast smugglers; nostalgic Galvanized “clubs” | Paper trails, jealous merchants |
| **Industrial runoff / slag-adjunct** | Low (stretcher) | Free or cheap; flavors the **Homan** crowd expects | Tanks **Reputation** with pure-human buyers; caps margin |

**Restaurant-style constraint:** Track **slots** (e.g., 3 “premium” pulls per week). Running out mid-service forces substitutions → patron checks, refunds, or **Heat** if you improvise with obvious magic.

### A.1 Experiment runs (prep night)

Use between sessions or **before doors open** when the crew wants to **tinker with the recipe** instead of running a safe, known batch. **Cost:** By default, each run **consumes one pull** per ingredient with dial **≥1** (heavy dials might cost **2** pulls — match your economy). *Light-touch option:* one **experiment** = **two total ingredient pulls** spread however you like, same dials and DCs.

**Input dials:** For **Vitalic-Amrita**, **Old World grain / malt**, and **Industrial runoff / slag-adjunct**, assign a level **0** (none in this trial) · **1** (standard) · **2** (heavy). At least **one** ingredient must be **1+** or the kettle isn’t doing science.

| Sum of dials (1–6) | Kettle strain | Suggested check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1–2** | cautious | DC **12** Intelligence (Alchemy), or brewer’s / cook’s tools |
| **3–4** | ambitious | DC **15** |
| **5–6** | reckless | DC **18**; on a **natural 1**, also roll **Mishap** (below) even if the check would succeed |

**Advantage / help:** A second PC can **Help** (grant advantage) if they describe a concrete prep step (stir timing, slag skim, grain toast). **Dr. Mire Cault**–style “waste” tips might lower the DC by **1** once per arc — strings attached.

**Outcome (after the check):**

| Result | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fail by 5+** | **Spoil** — lose the spent ingredients; no batch for tonight unless you fall back to a **non-experiment** brew pass (extra time / **Heat** at your discretion). Roll **Mishap**. |
| **Fail by 1–4** | **Wild ferment** — batch is **usable** but **unstable**: roll **Output** with **disadvantage** on the **2d6** (roll three dice, drop highest). |
| **Success** | Roll **Output** (2d6 + half proficiency bonus, rounded down, if you want heroes to feel skilled). |
| **Success by 5+** or **natural 20** | Roll **Output** with **advantage** on the **2d6** (roll three dice, drop lowest). |

**Output (2d6)**

| Total | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **2–3** | **Dump** — tastes wrong or tests hot; **lose ingredients**; no Veins Cut tonight unless you pivot to a normal brew pass. |
| **4–5** | **Thin Cut** — sells, but treat as **low margin**: −1 to **Charisma** checks to **upsell** or impress **VIP / Homan** tables this service (patrons feel the stretch). |
| **6–8** | **House Cut** — normal batch; no special modifier. |
| **9–10** | **Tagged batch** — pick or roll a **profile**: *Homan-forward* (big spenders happy), *Retro* (Galvanized **Intel** trades easier), or *Slag-bite* (Homans love it; **pure-human** buyers may **lose Rep** unless you spin it). One profile applies until the next experiment or until you **dump** the stock. |
| **11–12** | **Named variant** — player-facing name (e.g. **Homan High**, **Retro-Mojo**); **+1** to **Revenue** *or* **Reputation** this service (pick one). If **Kessa** or a **Successor hanger-on** is in play, they **notice** — good or bad. |

**Mishap (d6)** — roll on any **natural 1** on the strain check, **fail by 5+**, or when you need a sharp consequence.

| d6 | Mishap |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Short batch** — random event vibe: lose **1** extra ingredient unit or **Security** for the night (shield flicker, clogged mesh). |
| **2** | **Wrong nose** — **Penny-Watch** or a snitch gets a **lead**; **+1 Heat** unless someone spends **Intel** or **gold** to bury it. |
| **3** | **Resonance kiss** — faint **anomalous resonance** ping; **+1 Heat** if magic was used in the same room this scene. |
| **4** | **Bad rumor** — a patron spreads that the Cut “went sour”; **−1 Reputation** until you serve a **Tagged** or **Named** batch. |
| **5** | **Union interest** — **Dr. Cault** or a **Protective Custody** rumor **surfaces** next session (not necessarily a raid — a **sample** ask). |
| **6** | **Double trouble** — roll twice on this table; if both are **6**, the kettle **cracks** — no experiments until **repaired** (downtime + **shunts**). |

**Tie-in:** A **Named variant** from **Output** is the same mechanical slot as the **daily special** in **§B** — you’re just earning it through **experiment** instead of declaring it flat.

### B. The Brew Pass (Production)

*Skills: Intelligence (Alchemy), tool proficiency (brewer’s supplies / cook’s utensils), or approved spellwork (see Heat)*

- **Batch size:** Tie to crew size — one “kettle action” might cover X covers (patrons) for the night.
- **Daily special:** One **Veins Cut** line extension (e.g., **Homan High** — amrita + runoff; **Retro-Mojo** — smuggled grain, “Surface story” optional garnish). Declare it outright, or **discover** it via **§A.1 Output**. Specials can grant **minor tomorrow buffs** for regulars if you want a mechanical carrot.
- **Spoilage / contamination:** Failed checks waste ingredients and may force **dumping** (lost gold) or **serving risky stock** (patron incident table).

### C. Service (Front of house — management sim)

**Heat meter:** Loud wins (VIP blowouts, obvious magic, brawls) and **anomalous resonance** incidents push **Heat**. If **Heat** exceeds your front’s **Security** (Faraday shielding, bribes, forged permits, loyal lookouts), trigger a **Bad Event** (raid, snitch, “Protective Custody” sweep).

**Stations (action economy):**

| Station | Role | Typical checks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kettle / prep** | Finish batches, plate “meals,” label bottles | Alchemy, tools; burning a **spell component** can salvage a round but risks **resonance** |
| **Door / floor** | Seat, upsell, calm Homans, spot uniforms | Charisma (Deception, Persuasion), Insight |
| **Runner / stash** | Move product from shielded storage to service | Stealth, Sleight of Hand; **Volt-Stalker** timing matters |
| **Whisperer** | Intel instead of pouring | Investigation, Perception; overhear **Kinetic Front**, **smuggler** routes, **succession** and **cartel** gossip |

### D. Patron stack (demand cards)

Run 3–5 “tables” per round if you want crunch; each has **Demand**, **Patience**, and **Lore/Heat** hooks tied to the Veins.

| Patron | Orders | Patience | Lore / complication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nostalgic Galvanized** | Comfort + Old World ale | 3 | Trades **Intel** for Surface stories; usually **low Heat** |
| **Homan Hedonist** | 2+ “potions” / spiked rounds | 1–2 | High spend; rowdiness → **Heat**; overdose risk |
| **Surface Smuggler** | High-grade meal + “shunt” (barter chip) | 4 | Can discount **next procurement** if honored |
| **Penny-Watch / “helper”** | Something ordinary | 2 | Undercover or bribable **Volt-Stalker** — fail social → **+2 Heat** |
| **Successor hanger-on** | Private “tasting” | 1 | Claims to speak for **ACC**, a **cartel**, or a **provisional** boss — “protection” with paperwork or a **sample** quota |

## V. Law Enforcement & Evasion (Always On)

You are not hiding from “generic cops” — you are dodging **a city-sized lab leash**.

- **Protective Custody:** Framed as care; actually **intake for Union research**. A raid isn’t always arrest — sometimes it’s **medical pickup** with paperwork.
- **Anomalous resonance:** Visible or careless **Electro-Vitalic magic** is a spotlight. Prefer mundane chemistry, shielded rooms, or **Kinetic Front** safe-house rules.
- **Volt-Stalkers:** Mix of **sympathetic** (can warn, look away for a price) and **company men**. Reward players who **identify** which table they’re at.
- **Rumor weather:** The old **information blackout** is **fraying** — some blame the PCs for the killing, some celebrate, some just price-gouge. Leaning on **Overworld** talk can turn a room **fast**.

**Evasion tools (earn in play):** Faraday **skinning** upgrades, smuggler **routes**, Front **cover stories**, **Healing Center** contacts (non-tracked care if someone ODs), **bribes** timed to Penny-Watch shifts.

| Currency | From | Use in Faradhaven |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shunts / circuits (gold)** | Fulfilled orders, upsells | Ingredients, rent, **Security** upgrades |
| **Reputation** | VIP service, legend | Unlocks **Kinetic Front** meets, better smuggler rates — often **raises Heat** |

**End-of-night leash (optional):** If Julian Vane still holds strings, a **Vitalic-Amrita** pickup or debrief closes the loop — high **gold** pleases him; high **Intel** buys questions about **lost memories**, **ACC**, or who moves next in the Veins.

## VII. Random Events: “Copper Dirge” (when Heat > Security)

Roll **1d6** (or pick to match faction pressure):

1. **Short circuit:** Spike kills the batch — lost inventory, angry Homans.
2. **Penny-Watch raid:** Loud, sloppy sweep — **bribe**, **hide the kettle**, or lose product.
3. **Homan fluctuation:** **Vital Spark** crisis — needs mundane aid or a **Healing Center** run (magic draws **resonance**).
4. **Vacuum envoy:** Someone with **ACC** letterhead, **cartel** muscle, or a **provisional** seal demands a back-room deal — **partnership**, **leverage**, or a **Protective Custody** threat dressed as “stability.”
5. **Kinetic Front:** Wants the front as a **safe** meeting room — **Heat** vs. **allies**.
6. **Static haze:** Collective **memory bleed** (Civil War echo) — disadvantage on service checks until grounded.

## VIII. DM Quick Prompts

- Which **faction** owns the block tonight (nostalgia street fair vs. Homan club crawl)?
- What **ingredient** is late, and who is holding it hostage?
- Is tonight’s **Veins Cut** a safe **House Cut**, or are they running an **experiment** with dials cranked?
- Is the **Volt-Stalker** at the door the one you paid last week?
- Does a **Surface** smuggler want a cut, or a favor that touches the **Overworld**?
- Who is **claiming** the old **Mayor’s conduit** this week — **Greff**, **Cault**, **Cartwright**, or a **Rook** with a stolen seal?
- Does **Kessa** show up for a **tasting**, or **Sister Coil** call in a favor from a past **Healing Center** save?

Keep the camera on **Copper Veins**: brass, rumor, recycled glamour, and the brew as the thing that forces the PCs to touch every stratum — while **every faction** tests whether the killers are **assets**, **liabilities**, or **prey**.
