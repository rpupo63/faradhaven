This document compares the current "Welcome to Axiom" introduction page with the detailed world-building provided in the Q&A session. It identifies **Naming Differences** and **Missing Concepts** (key plot points, mechanics, or setting details absent from the intro).

## 2. Missing Concepts & Mechanics

The following substantial concepts from the Q&A are **completely missing** from the current introduction:

### A. The Underground Society & Economy
*   **Gap:** The intro implies the characters are unique "prototypes" waking up in a lab.
*   **Q&A Reality:** There is an entire **hidden underclass of Homunculi** living underground. They have their own society, trading in **Copper and Gold** (conductive metals) because they are rejected by the surface world (which uses "Faradollars").

### B. The Homunculus Ecology (Evolution)
*   **Gap:** The intro mentions "Electro-Sovereign" status (generating own current).
*   **Q&A Reality:** Homunculi have a biological imperative to **evolve**. They can do this by **consuming other Homunculi** or besting them in combat. This adds a predatory/survivalist layer to their existence that is not currently mentioned.

### C. The Religious Conspiracy (Cyborgism)
*   **Gap:** The intro mentions "Trans-Electric Ascendancy" as a vague goal.
*   **Q&A Reality:** There is a specific **"Cyborgism" Faith** (The Old Guard/Cult branches) that seeks to **build a God from electricity**. Crucially, the Student's **Mentor** is involved in this cult, suggesting the main characters might be intended as vessels for this deity, not just products.

### D. The Transit Conspiracy
*   **Gap:** The intro says the elite are powered by the "Tether-Line" and travel is cheap.
*   **Q&A Reality:** The **ETC (Elevated Transit Company)** runs the trains. The Q&A hints that the "suspiciously cheap" transit might be a **harvesting grid**, siphoning vital energy from passengers to power the Zeppelins above.

### E. The Civil War & The North's Tech
*   **Gap:** The intro mentions "Northern Hegemony" and "Field of Fused Bone" vaguely.
*   **Q&A Reality:** The bodies used to make the characters are specifically from the **Civil War** between North and South. The North (Unionists) isn't just "fearful"; they are developing **Anti-Tech Generators** (two copper ends) specifically designed to drain the life force from electro-vitalic beings.

### F. Mission Specifics
*   **Gap:** The intro says "Ensure they fail."
*   **Q&A Reality:** The mission involves a **DCC Bionic Arm Exhibition** (outdoor/convention style). The specific sabotage targets are **Cables**, the **Controller**, or the **Test User**.

## 3. Summary of Missing Elements

The following key narrative elements are present in the Q&A lore but completely absent from the current introduction:

*   **A. The Underground Society & Economy:** The existence of a hidden Homunculus underclass that trades in conductive metals (Copper/Gold) and lives in the "No-Go" zones underground.
*   **B. The Homunculus Ecology:** The biological imperative for Homunculi to evolve by consuming others or through combat, adding a survivalist dynamic.
*   **C. The Religious Conspiracy:** The "Cyborgism" faith and the Mentor's secret goal to build a God from electricity using the prototypes.
*   **D. The Transit Conspiracy:** The dark implication that the ETC transit system harvests vital energy from passengers to power the elite's Zeppelins.
