# World Note: Faradhaven

## **I. Geography & Atmosphere: The Industrial Gothic**

Faradhaven is the capital of **South Axiom**, a breakaway state defined by rapid industrialization and **Ichor-Current**. The city exists in a state of **Galvanic Gothic** architecture, where old brick is encased in **Faraday Skinnings** (copper overlays) to siphon energy from threats.

- **The Static Haze:** A permanent, thick "pea-soup" fog that chokes the streets.
- **The Copper Dirge:** The sonic identity of the city. Industrial zones clank with pistons while copper scaffolding and **Conductive Spires** whistle, moan, and vibrate with resonant energy.
- **The Slag-Pits & The Hum:** The poor reside in the **Slag-Pits** near factory zones, while the middle class occupies **The Hum** at the city center.
- **The Cloud-Citadels:** The ultra-rich live in **Aerostat Estates** (suspended zeppelins), connected by the **Tether-Line**—a transit network that suspiciously doubles as an energy harvester.
- **The Dead-Circuit Plains:** A haunted battlefield 10 miles out that serves as the primary entrance to **The Sub-Station** (the Underground), where the outcasts dwell.

---

## **II. Science & Religion: The Vital Spark**

The world is powered by **Electro-Vitalism**, the extraction of the **Vital Spark** from living or deceased matter.

- **The Trans-Electric Ascendancy:** A cyborgism faith seeking to build a mechanical God.
- **The Galvanized:** The technical term for homunculi—modified humans or generated beings who form the underclass of **The Sub-Station**. They evolve by honing skills or consuming the Ichor-Current of others.
- **The Over-Clocked:** Failed experiments or black-market enforcers that have gone mad, often referred to as "monsters" by the public.
- **The Luddite Inquisition:** The "Old Guard" of the North who view this tech as a moral rot. They use copper-tipped generators to "un-ground" the Galvanized, causing rapid decay.

---

## **III. Economics & Power: The Current Cartels**

Faradhaven is ruled by profit. **The Penny-Watch** (police) is well-meaning but utterly ineffective due to underfunding.

| :----------- | :--------------------------- | :----------------------------------------- |
| **DCC**      | **Direct Flow Syndicate**    | Human bionic/vitalic augmentation.         |
| **ACC**      | **The Oscillating Union**    | Weapon and item enhancements.              |
| **ETC**      | **The Tether-Line Co.**      | Mass transit and zeppelin power supply.    |
| **Triple P** | **The Volt-Stalkers**        | Illegally modified private enforcers.      |
| **ECC**      | **Evolutionary Current Co.** | The PC creator's startup for "New Beings." |

- **Currency:** The **Sovereign Volt** is the overworld standard. The Underground uses **Shunts** (copper) and **Circuits** (gold) for their conductivity.

---

## **IV. The Protagonists: Julian Vane’s Thesis**

The main characters are **The Galvanized**, scavenged from **The Dead-Circuit Plains** and reanimated by a university prodigy, **Julian Vane**.

- **The Thesis:** _"The Self-Sustaining Circuit: A Study in Post-Mortem Volition."_ Vane aims to create "Electro-Sovereign" beings who generate their own current through cellular friction.
- **The Leash:** Vane maintains control through a daily injection of **Vitalic-Amrita**. He claims this "repair" is necessary to stop their nightly decay, though it may actually be a **limiter-inhibitor** to keep them from realizing their true power.
- **The Leverage:** Vane has siphoned their memories from the Civil War and holds physical artifacts (or people) from their past lives as collateral.
- **The Awareness:** As they evolve, the PCs feel a magnetic pull toward the city center, hearing the **Copper Dirge** as a literal compass leading toward a "Great Field."

---

## **V. Current Objectives**

1.  **Sabotage:** The PCs must disrupt a **Direct Flow Syndicate** public demonstration of a bionic arm to prove Vane’s **Evolutionary Current Co.** is the superior market disruptor.
2.  **Survival:** Manage the nightly "Memory Overlap" where the sensory horrors of the Civil War return as the **Vital Spark** fluctuates.
3.  **Conflict:** Avoid the **Kinetic Front** (Techno-Revolutionaries) and the **Northern Hegemony** (Unionists) while navigating the corporate warfare of the Cartels.
