# World Note: The Copper Veins (The Sub-Station)

## **I. Political Infrastructure: The Mayor’s Office**

**The Copper Veins** are governed by a figurehead known simply as **The Mayor**. While he presents himself as a populist revolutionary, his administration is a carefully constructed corporate facade.

- **Controlled Opposition:** The Mayor maintains his position by "authorizing" minor, non-violent acts of industrial sabotage against the Overworld. This gives the residents a sense of agency while preventing any actual threat to the **Current Cartels**.
- **The Information Blackout:** The Mayor’s primary directive is to foster deep-seated xenophobia toward the Overworld. By making the Surface seem like a source of pure hatred and doom, he ensures that the **Galvanized** never attempt to integrate, thus preventing public research into the ethics of the Underground.
- **The ACC Conduit:** Behind the scenes, the Mayor’s office acts as a field laboratory for **The Oscillating Union (ACC)**. He provides "live samples" (Galvanized citizens) to corporate scientists in exchange for the political backing and supplies needed to maintain his regime.

## **II. Social Strata & Factions**

The social fabric of the Veins is defined by how its inhabitants perceive their own humanity—or lack thereof.

### **1. The "Temporarily Embarrassed Humans"**

This faction is composed of the **Galvanized** who view their current state as a temporary affliction. They are the keepers of "Old World" nostalgia.

- **Culture:** They obsessively recreate human traditions from a decade prior—the era just before the "Vitalic Revolution."
- **The Retro-Mojo:** Their districts are filled with outdated fashions and recreations of 90s-era human social norms, serving as a psychological anchor to a life they hope to one day reclaim.

### **2. The Homans**

The Homans have rejected humanity entirely, declaring themselves a superior, post-human species.

- **The Abstinent Hedonists:** Because they are biologically incapable of reproduction, their culture has evolved into a strict anti-sex, anti-intimacy society.
- **Mixology & Potions:** To replace traditional biological drives, they focus on "The Party." Homan Mixologists are celebrated figures who spike **Vitalic-Amrita** with chemical runoff and industrial byproducts. These "Potions" are highly toxic to humans but provide the Homans with intense, erratic bursts of the **Vital Spark**.

### **3. The Surface-Outcasts (Pure Humans)**

A minority population of "pure" humans who have fled to the Veins due to debt, crime, or political exile.

- **The Smugglers:** Because they lack the necrotic visible markers of the Galvanized, they can "pass" on the Overworld. This allows them to control the flow of high-grade medical supplies and non-toxic food.
- **Friction:** They occupy an uneasy middle ground, often acting as the ruling merchants of the Underground while maintaining a deep-seated sense of biological superiority over the "Synthetics."

---

## **III. Judicial "Correction" & Research**

Law enforcement in the Veins is designed to funnel citizens back into corporate research cycles.

- **Protective Custody:** The "jail" system in the Veins is marketed as a safe haven for the vulnerable. In reality, it is a processing center where the **Galvanized** are subjected to invasive testing by **The Oscillating Union**.
- **Magic & Evaluation:** A key area of research in the Veins is the manifestation of **Electro-Vitalic Magic**. The Mayor’s administration monitors the population for any "anomalous resonance" (magic use), which usually leads to immediate "permanent detention" for deeper study.

---

## **IV. The Underground Sanctuary**

Despite the Mayor’s surveillance, a clandestine network of **Volt-Stalkers** (sympathetic guards) and **Kinetic Front** operatives maintains "Safe Spaces."

- **The Healing Centers:** These are the only locations in the Veins where a Galvanized individual can receive medical attention that isn't rigged with **limiter-inhibitors** or trackers.
- **The Underground Resistance:** These safe houses serve as the true cultural centers for those who have seen through the Mayor’s corporate-sponsored "revolution."
